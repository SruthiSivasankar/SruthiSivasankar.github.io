About Me

My journey to Product Management

My non-traditional path began in architecture, where I learned to build around users. Experimenting with programming to simplify repetitive AEC workflows sparked a fascination to build software products. This curiosity led me to pursue a Master’s in Information Systems, where I learned to bridge engineering, AI and business.

Through hackathons, side projects, and startup environments, I found my passion lies not just in building apps, but in figuring out what to build and why. I obsess over small details, turning ambiguous problems and unconventional ideas into thoughtful product by connecting user needs, technology, business goals.
