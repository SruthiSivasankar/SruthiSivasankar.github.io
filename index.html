About Me

My journey to Software Engineering

I didn’t get into software through the traditional path. I got into it through curiosity. What started as experimenting with programming to simplify repetitive workflows in architecture slowly turned into a passion for building software, eventually leading me to pursue a Master’s in Information Systems. Since then, I’ve been exploring full-stack systems, AI-driven applications, cloud and

Product development through side projects, hackathons, and startup-style environments where you learn as you build. My favorite experiences have come from fast-paced teams with different backgrounds building things that didn’t exist earlier. I also love exploring new technologies, prototyping random ideas, and obsessing over small product details.
