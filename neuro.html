Neuro.Explorer - A BCI Odyssey

Gamification of Brain Computer Interface Studies

The Vision

Making Complex Technology Understandable

Brain-computer interfaces (BCI) connect brain activity with computer systems, but understanding how they work can require familiarity with neuroscience, signal processing, and machine learning. The broader idea was to make an emerging technology feel less intimidating and more understandable. An everyday applications and games can help bring wider public awareness and investments. Thus, making complex technologies accessible.

Neuro.Explorer explores a more approachable entry point to BCI technology: conceptualized an interactive iOS game adventure that introduces the technology through a tangible, relatable mission rather than technical explanations alone.

The narrative follows an explorer (Players) helping Persona Alex, a nonspeaking character, traveling to build a BCI system to communicate. Scientific concepts become part of the journey, and completing challenges reinforce the stages involved in a BCI pipeline, culminating in the explorer returning with conceptually developed system.

The Concept Development & Technical Exploration

Breaking down the BCI process into the concepts a beginner would need to understand to see how neural activity can become a computer-controlled action. And structure the adventure around key stages:

  • Signal acquisition: How brain activity is captured through approaches such as EEG and other invasive or non-invasive methods.
  • Signal processing & interpretation: How raw signals are processed to identify patterns associated with intended actions, communication, or other target signals.
  • Training & pattern recognition: How collected data is used to train a system to recognize relevant patterns.
  • Control systems & application: How interpreted signals are translated into commands that control an external device or enable communication.
  • BCI equipment & development: The tools and components involved in acquiring signals, processing them, training models, and connecting outputs to a control interface.

These concepts provided the blueprint for the adventure, with each gameplay mechanic designed around a specific part of the BCI system, giving each stage its own environment, objective, and interaction.

Experience Design & Game Mechanics

Structured the journey around core BCI components, brainwave signals, data acquisition, signal processing, and system control. Quizzes unlock new locations, while each gameplay mechanic introduces a different part of the BCI process:

  • Brainwave Navigation | Signal Acquisition & Identification: A rhythm-style challenge where players jump only on identified beta waves. This introduces the idea of brainwave patterns, signal identification, and data acquisition.
  • Equipment Collection | BCI Components & System Understanding: A projectile-based game where players identify and collect relevant BCI components as part of the experience, reinforcing the equipment and tools involved in building a system.
  • Signal Pattern Labyrinth | Training & Pattern Recognition: Players match brainwave patterns to corresponding emotion categories to clear obstacles in maze, representing the simplified concepts of training models with brainwave data, distinguish patterns and recognition, and signal interpretation.
  • Quizzes & Location Unlocks | Concept Reinforcement: Short quizzes check the player's understanding before the adventure progresses. Correct answers unlock new locations, linking the individual activities into a sequence for conceptual learning rather than a collection of unrelated mini-games to progress through the adventure.
  • Communication Finale | Control & Application: The adventure culminates in helping Alex communicate, connecting signal acquisition, interpretation, and system control to the broader purpose of BCI technology. Thus, game mechanics become holistic educational representations of BCI principles

Interface Design & Visual World

Designed the iOS interface and visual presentation to support the explorer narrative and distinguish application screens from interactive game scenes. Used SwiftUI for interface layouts and navigation, with Photoshop assets and SpriteKit scene elements to create the visual environments, interactive objects, and animated challenges.

Designing & Engineering Challenge

The project brought together several distinct engineering challenges: coordinating SwiftUI screens with SpriteKit scenes, managing interactive physics and animation, and maintaining a coherent experience across multiple gameplay environments.

The central design challenge was deciding how to represent complex BCI concepts in an introductory, simplified form and mapping scientific concepts to intuitive interactions without losing their underlying meaning. By giving each BCI stage a distinct mechanic, I connected learning objectives, player actions, and feedback within one coherent experience.

This approach demonstrated how technical complexity can be made more approachable through thoughtful design and engineering.

Development Insight

Application Engineering

Built the application by combining declarative interface development with a dedicated 2D game engine.

  • SwiftUI: Built the application interface, screen layouts, navigation, and presentation.
  • SpriteKit: Developed 2D gameplay, scenes, physics-based collision handling to manage game objects interactions, animations, object movement, and projectile interactions and mechanics.
  • SpriteView Integration: Embedded interactive SpriteKit scenes within SwiftUI layouts.
  • Combine: Coordinated asynchronous events, data flow, and recurring events.
  • SpriteNodes & SKTexture: Managed interactive objects, textures, positioning, scaling, and movement throughout the game world.
  • Visual Assets: Created the visual experience and custom scene composition using Photoshop assets.
